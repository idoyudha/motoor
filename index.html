What Motoor does

One logbook for everything you drive and ride

No spreadsheets, no shoebox of receipts. Log it once and Motoor does the counting.

{% include icon.html name="bell" %}

Service reminders

Reminders by distance or by date, whichever comes first. Motoor tells you what’s overdue, what’s due soon and what can wait.

{% include icon.html name="fuel" %}

Fuel and charging

Log each fill in a few taps. Motoor works out your consumption and what each fill costs you.

{% include icon.html name="coin" %}

Costs that add up

Service, parts, tax, parking and insurance, all in one place. See what each vehicle really costs per mile or per month.

Full service history

Every service with its date, odometer, workshop and photos. Useful at the next service, and when you sell.
